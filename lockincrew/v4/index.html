six rules from the twilight.

this is where the light starts to go. so do the calendar invites.

001

locked in > logged on

presence is not output. we measure ourselves in shipped artifacts, not hours of green dot.

010

fewer rituals.

no daily standup. no retro about the retro. one written brief, one done thing, one demo.

011

the keyboard is the artifact.

strategy decks rot. running code compounds. we bias hard toward the latter.

100

small units, sharp scope.

two-to-four operators per cell. fixed window. brutal definition of done.

101

taste is non-negotiable.

fast is good. fast & tasteful is the whole job. if it ships ugly, it didn't ship.

110

own the outcome.

we don't hand off tickets. we walk the thing into prod & stay until it breathes on its own.
